ELMNT EARTH began with ethically sourced Palo Santo from Peru. It is now growing into a creative and wellbeing studio: a space to reconnect, create and simply be.

Where we started

Our Palo Santo is gathered only from naturally fallen wood in the dry forests of Piura and hand pressed in Huanchaco, on Peru's northern coast. Break · Burn · Breathe has been our ritual from the beginning. Read the Palo Santo story.

Where we're going

The brand remains. The way it works is changing. Alongside our remaining Palo Santo collection, ELMNT EARTH is now home to three things:

  • Meditate: free guided breathing meditations, a new one every week.
  • Reconnect: Reiki and energy sessions.
  • Create: websites and back-end setup for sole traders and small businesses.

Our poetry book Elmnts is available on Amazon, and handmade INTI pieces are in the shop.

The shop is closing

Meditations and Reiki will continue through ELMNT Earth, but the retail shop is closing. All remaining products are 60% off storewide, while stock lasts.
