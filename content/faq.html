Where is your Palo Santo sourced?

Our Palo Santo is handcrafted by Wanchako in Huanchaco, northern Peru. Every product is made using naturally fallen Palo Santo wood, following traditional methods that honour both the forest and the local communities.

Is your Palo Santo ethically sourced?

Yes. Our Palo Santo comes only from naturally fallen trees and branches. No living trees are cut down. Harvesting is regulated under Peru's SERFOR forestry authority, helping to protect the species and preserve its natural ecosystem.

Why choose ELMNT EARTH?

We work directly with Wanchako, a family-owned producer in Peru, to bring you authentic Palo Santo of exceptional quality. Every product is thoughtfully created with respect for tradition, sustainability and ritual.

Do you cut down living Palo Santo trees?

No. Genuine Palo Santo should never come from living trees. The wood is collected only after trees or branches have naturally fallen and naturally matured, allowing the rich aroma to fully develop before harvesting.

What is SERFOR?

SERFOR (Servicio Nacional Forestal y de Fauna Silvestre) is Peru's National Forestry and Wildlife Service. It regulates the sustainable harvesting and export of Palo Santo to help protect Peru's forests for future generations.

Why do you partner with Wanchako?

We travelled to Peru to meet the people behind our products and chose to work with Wanchako because of their commitment to sustainability, traditional craftsmanship and ethical sourcing. It's a partnership built on shared values and trust.

Does purchasing your products support local communities?

Yes. By working directly with Wanchako, your purchase helps support local artisans, ethical employment and sustainable forestry initiatives in northern Peru.

What's the difference between the Palo Santo Incense and the Aura Mist?

Our Palo Santo Incense is handcrafted using pure milled Palo Santo wood, creating a traditional aromatic smoke for cleansing, meditation and ritual.

Our Palo Santo Aura Mist is made with authentic Peruvian Palo Santo essential oil, offering a convenient smoke-free alternative to refresh your body, home, workspace or linens.
